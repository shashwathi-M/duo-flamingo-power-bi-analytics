## 📌 Project Overview

DuoFlamingo is a business intelligence project focused on SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) subscription and business performance analytics.

The project aims to develop an interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard that enables a fictional subscription-based business to monitor recurring revenue, subscription growth, customer retention, billing performance and payment operations.

The proposed analytical platform will transform structured business data into meaningful insights to support management decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) and subscription growth.
- Analyse customer retention and subscription cancellations.
- Evaluate revenue contributions across subscription plans.
- Examine customer distribution across geographical regions and acquisition channels.
- Monitor invoice collection and payment success rates.
- Develop actionable business recommendations using data analytics.

## ⚠️ Dataset Limitations

This project uses synthetic educational data representing a fictional subscription business. The dataset does not contain free-user activity, trial-user records, marketing expenditure or customer acquisition costs.

Therefore, the project focuses on subscription revenue, retention, churn, billing and payment analytics. Results will be interpreted as illustrative findings rather than actual commercial business performance.
